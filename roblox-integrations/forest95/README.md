# TreeWorld version 95 — cottage, terrain and individual radio

Universe 9421311218; place 118064584465155; base published version 94.

The lake cottage is reconstructed with 200 visible construction pieces: porch planks supply their own collision, submerged piles reach measured terrain, the west doorway faces the boardwalk, roof slopes meet at one ridge and end gables close the attic. The original cottage tea corner is preserved and moved by the table's actual bottom bounds rather than a model pivot, fixing floor penetration after serialization. Its four chairs and four direct-click cups remain. The rebuilt 21-segment boardwalk has 189 tested walking samples. Eighteen width/height rays check the entire entrance corridor.

Terrain smoothing uses the saved native heightfield and 7x7 weighted neighborhoods, with geometry preserved around ruins, camps, hill stairs and bridge endpoints. Native liquid occupancy is retained. 22,397 dry columns are smoothed and 969 path columns receive a pale sand material. 96 decorative rock models are removed, leaving 46 accent rocks, plus the retained ten faceted river crossing stones. Seventy tall trees are limited to 68 studs. Trees retain their source meshes and PBR, with moderately greener leaves, and every tree/plant is regrounded after terrain changes. Standard grass remains native and short at 0.4; no custom grass meshes are added. Four separated ruins, four compact tea tables, seasonal changes, forest sounds, falling leaves and weather are retained.

The original radio model 15855506991 is imported with its eight native meshes, placed on an indoor shelf. Both globally audible source Sounds and its original caption Script are removed. A responsive scrollable personal music panel opens from the top-right Music button or the radio prompt. It offers seven verified presets (two tracks catalogued in English albums, acoustic/nature-inspired music, forest birds and stream sound), an audio-ID field, play/pause/stop and volume controls. Audio instances are created exclusively from the local player's client in SoundService. A dedicated remote receives a boolean listening state only; it neither receives nor broadcasts IDs, track names or audio. The server adds the exact fixed listening label to the player's head tag. Pause, stop or a failed load clears that state. Bad/custom unavailable IDs display an availability message; native platform audio permissions are respected.

Testing includes native audio loading of every preset and two separate player-core instances: pausing and destroying A leaves B playing. This is an isolated engine test, not a rendered two-client network session. The UI factory is instantiated and checked for scrolling, text input and control wiring. World geometry is 10,282 parts, down from 10,455. All 7,781 saved support contacts, 855 bridge samples, 45 central lake water samples, 30 stone top samples, tea cup delivery, finite world geometry and script compilation passed staging.

No actual Roblox client render, exact image equality, English lyric transcription, phone FPS or phone memory measurement is available. Some reference images are from other engines; geometry, materials and layout are adapted to Roblox. Native root collision queries and placed bounds are used for support. No external shader injector is installed.
